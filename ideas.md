# Sobha Realty Replica — Design Ground Truth

This is a reference-replication project. The live Sobha Realty homepage at https://sobharealty.com/ and the supplied mobile screenshots are the authoritative visual specification; fidelity takes precedence over introducing a new visual direction.

## Reference Specification

The experience is a luxury real-estate editorial site built around full-bleed architectural imagery, a transparent overlay header, restrained white typography, and slow, cinematic transitions. The mobile landing state uses a full-height aerial development image, a darkened gradient for legibility, a compact header with hamburger/logo/wishlist/search, and a lower-third hero lockup consisting of a serif project name, one-line descriptor, and letter-spaced pill CTA. The mobile menu is a full-viewport black overlay with a thin gray left edge, back and close controls, and vertically stacked uppercase navigation labels with right-aligned chevrons.

The desktop reference adds a wider transparent nav with left and right link clusters around a centered wordmark, a hero carousel, a content transition into brand/craftsmanship storytelling, a community/property carousel, and a press-release section. Scroll behavior should feel continuous and cinematic: hero imagery subtly drifts, content reveals on entry, cards slide horizontally, and navigation overlays enter/exit with opacity and transform transitions. Motion must be disabled or reduced for users who request reduced motion.

## Design Movement

Contemporary luxury editorialism with a cinematic property-film sensibility.

## Core Principles

1. **Image-first storytelling:** architectural imagery occupies the largest visual field and text remains deliberately sparse.
2. **Quiet precision:** thin rules, restrained controls, and generous negative space convey confidence rather than decoration.
3. **Material contrast:** black, charcoal, soft white, and muted champagne tones create a tactile luxury atmosphere.
4. **Scroll as narrative:** each section should feel like the next frame of a property film, with measured reveals and directional transitions.

## Color Philosophy

The palette is anchored in near-black (#080808), warm white (#F4F1EB), and muted stone gray, with a restrained champagne accent inspired by the reference wordmark. Dark surfaces frame imagery and preserve the premium mood; white type is used only where the image treatment guarantees contrast.

## Layout Paradigm

A full-bleed, vertical editorial composition rather than a centered marketing grid. Desktop sections may use asymmetrical split layouts and horizontal rails; mobile collapses into tall filmic panels, with content aligned to the lower third and edges rather than centered in card stacks.

## Signature Elements

The site repeats a thin white header rule, letter-spaced uppercase microcopy, and pill-shaped translucent CTAs over imagery. The menu overlay is solid black and typographically strict. Subtle vignette/gradient treatments make each hero image read as a cinematic frame.

## Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should feel composed and physical: buttons compress slightly on press, carousel navigation glides rather than snaps, and the menu opens as a purposeful black curtain. Hover states are quiet and editorial; no bright color bursts or excessive bouncing.

## Animation

Use opacity and transform-based entry reveals with 500–900ms cinematic easing, subtle image scale/parallax in hero panels, staggered text lines, and 180–280ms control transitions. Horizontal property rails should glide with inertial-feeling easing. All non-essential motion is gated by prefers-reduced-motion.

## Typography System

Use a high-contrast serif display face for project names and section titles, paired with a clean sans-serif for navigation, labels, and body copy. Display titles are large, light-weight, and carefully tracked; navigation is uppercase, bold enough for contrast, and widely letter-spaced; CTAs use compact uppercase microcopy.

## Brand Essence

A cinematic digital showroom for people seeking exceptionally crafted homes, distinguished by calm luxury and architectural storytelling. Personality: **composed, exacting, cinematic**.

## Brand Voice

Headlines are evocative and concise; CTAs are direct, never salesy; microcopy is understated and assured. Example lines: “Spaces Where Life Unfolds.” and “Discover a quieter way to live.”

## Wordmark & Logo

Use a compact SOBHA-style wordmark treatment with wide tracking, a small REALTY subline, and a restrained champagne/white accent mark. On mobile it remains centered and compact; on dark overlays it is white with a subtle warm accent.

## Signature Brand Color

Muted champagne gold: #C9B48A.
