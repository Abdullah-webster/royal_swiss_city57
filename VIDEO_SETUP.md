# Mobile Hero Video Setup

The mobile hero background is configured to use a local video file at `/public/hero-mobile-bg.mp4`

## To add your video:

1. Download a stock video (recommended 30-60 seconds, 16:9 or 9:16 aspect ratio)
   - Free options: Pexels, Pixabay, Unsplash Videos
   - Recommended format: MP4 (H.264 codec)
   - Recommended resolution: 1080p or higher
   - Recommended size: 10-30MB

2. Place the video file at: `client/public/hero-mobile-bg.mp4`

3. The video will automatically:
   - Auto-play on mobile
   - Loop infinitely
   - Mute audio
   - Fit the entire screen (objectFit: cover)
   - Work on all mobile browsers

Example stock video sources:
- Pexels Videos: https://www.pexels.com/videos/
- Pixabay Videos: https://pixabay.com/videos/
- Unsplash Videos: https://unsplash.com/napi/videos/

Just download any urban/cityscape or riverfront video and save it as `hero-mobile-bg.mp4` in the public folder!
