## 1. COMPANY INTRODUCTION, PRESENCE & SOCIAL LINKS

### Habib Rafiq (Pvt.) Limited

Habib Rafiq (Pvt.) Limited (HRL) is a leading Pakistan based engineering and construction company with a distinguished legacy spanning more than six decades, built on a strong foundation of customer satisfaction, professional excellence and the successful delivery of projects of national and international importance. This longstanding commitment to reliability, timely execution and professional service has established HRL as a trusted name within Pakistan’s engineering and construction landscape. Today, the company continues to build upon its 60 year legacy, bringing the same depth of expertise, uncompromising standards and proven capability that have defined its journey to the development of modern, future focused projects designed to create lasting value for generations to come.

# 2. COMPANY DETAILED INTRODUCTION

Habib Rafiq (Pvt.) Limited (HRL) has evolved from a long-established engineering and construction enterprise into a diversified, multi-sector contractor and project delivery organization, serving major public-sector, private-sector and international clients across a broad range of industries and infrastructure sectors.

Over the course of approximately six decades, HRL has built its reputation on the principles of **commitment, distinction and professional excellence**, undertaking projects of considerable scale and complexity that demand the seamless integration of civil, mechanical and electrical engineering capabilities. This multidisciplinary foundation has enabled the company to successfully deliver assignments of substantial engineering, economic and national significance.

Its extensive project portfolio demonstrates proven capability across:

* **Engineering, procurement and project management**
* **Civil construction and structural works**
* **Mechanical fabrication and erection**
* **Electrical, instrumentation and control-system installation**
* **Plant commissioning and start-up support**
* **Plant maintenance, operation and turnaround services**
* **Housing, land development and integrated community infrastructure**
* **Roads, motorways and associated infrastructure**
* **Commercial, institutional, residential and high-rise developments**
* **Airports and aviation facilities**
* **Power plants and energy infrastructure**
* **Chemical, petrochemical, fertilizer and process-industry facilities**
* **Oil refineries and oil and gas infrastructure**
* **Environmental, wastewater-treatment and sanitation infrastructure**
* **Water-resource engineering and associated utility networks**

HRL's continued development has also been marked by a transition from conventional construction practices towards **state-of-the-art, highly mechanized construction methodologies**. This evolution reflects the company's commitment to combining established engineering expertise with modern construction techniques, advanced equipment, disciplined project management and efficient execution practices to meet the increasingly complex demands of major projects.

The company's capabilities are not confined to any single engineering or construction discipline. Its documented projects frequently require the coordinated delivery of structural and civil works alongside mechanical systems, electrical and instrumentation installations, HVAC, fire protection, plumbing, utilities, roads, drainage, water infrastructure and other essential support systems. This integrated approach is particularly evident in large-scale EPC, infrastructure and industrial assignments, where effective coordination between multiple technical disciplines is critical to successful project delivery.

HRL also places strong emphasis on **timely project completion** as a defining element of its delivery philosophy. Its corporate material highlights a consistent focus on disciplined planning, effective execution and schedule management, including the successful completion of projects within, and in certain cases ahead of, agreed time frames.

### Core Organizational Strengths

#### Multidisciplinary Execution

HRL's portfolio demonstrates its ability to integrate and execute civil, mechanical and electrical scopes within a single project environment. This multidisciplinary capability allows the company to manage complex assignments through a coordinated approach, reducing fragmentation between technical disciplines and supporting greater control over project execution, quality and delivery.

#### Large-Scale Infrastructure Delivery

The company's experience extends to the development of major housing and infrastructure schemes incorporating extensive land development and integrated public-service networks. These projects include roads, water-supply systems, sewerage and drainage networks, electrification, street lighting, treatment facilities and other essential infrastructure required to support large-scale communities and developments.

#### Industrial and Process Expertise

HRL has developed substantial experience in the execution of complex industrial and process-sector projects, including construction and installation works for power plants, fertilizer facilities, cement-related systems, refineries and other major industrial developments. Such assignments require a high degree of technical coordination, specialist knowledge and rigorous control across multiple engineering disciplines.

#### International Project Experience

In addition to its extensive operations within Pakistan, HRL's corporate portfolio records project activity and experience beyond the country's borders, particularly within the Gulf region. Its documented international work also includes projects such as the **Middle & Low Cost Housing Scheme in Malaysia** and the **300-Bed Saudi German Hospital in Yemen**, reflecting the company's experience in delivering projects across different markets and operating environments.

#### Quality, Health, Safety & Environment

Quality, Health, Safety and Environment form an integral part of HRL's organizational approach to project execution. The company's certification and corporate material describe an organization-wide framework supported by defined systems, management structures and implementation across all operational levels, reinforcing a commitment to maintaining professional standards, safeguarding people and managing environmental responsibilities throughout the project lifecycle.
